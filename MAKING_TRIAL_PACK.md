## First reference build: gift box, recipe 1

Use one non-load-bearing craft sample. Finished fold-centerline target: width 90 mm, depth 60 mm, height 35 mm. Outer blank: 180×245 mm. Ribbon: 560 mm plus the separate layout cutting allowance. Four tabs: 10×35 mm. The net contains base/lid 90×60 mm, front/back 90×35 mm, left/right 35×60 mm. Tools: scissors, ruler and suitable craft glue; allow the adhesive’s stated drying time separately. Actual card thickness and fold/adhesive tolerances must be recorded.

1. Create a one-box plan with actual measured card/ribbon. Open Learn to make this → 2D guide → Print all steps. Check printed measurements with a ruler; redraw dimensions if scaling differs.
2. Photograph the uncut material alongside a ruler. Record thickness, usable rectangle and ribbon length. Keep tabs attached when cutting the outer net silhouette; do not cut through dashed hinges.
3. Score the five wall/lid hinges and four tab hinges. Dry-fit all walls at 90°, turn tabs inward and verify the lid reaches across the opening before glue.
4. Glue tabs against the side walls and let set according to the adhesive instructions. Close the lid and tie ribbon. Record any collisions, forced fit, unclear fold or insufficient ribbon; never silently trim and report a pass.
5. Photograph flat cut net, open assembled box, closed box and measured reusable offcuts. Measure finished dimensions. Record actual hands-on minutes separately from drying.
6. Enter actual results in the app. Mark recipe physically tested only after checking the observations and updating any tolerances, recipe version and regressions required by a failure.

## Novice trials

Recruit at least three willing people unfamiliar with this tutorial. Do not coach through the steps; record where help is required. Use participant codes rather than publishing personal information. Obtain permission before using their photos/video.

Tasks: find the tutorial from a plan; identify owned vs purchased materials; explain which cuts remain when outer-blank service is included; assemble one box; leave and resume a saved step; record good/spoiled pieces and one measured offcut. Include one person using 2D/keyboard or reduced motion.

For each participant record: recipe/size, device, start/end time, completed independently, steps needing help, incorrect cuts/folds, material waste, missing tools, resume success, subjective clarity 1–5 and verbatim improvement suggestions. Preserve failures and uncertainty alongside successes. Target: all participants can locate the tutorial and identify required tools without coaching; revise unclear steps before claiming usability.
