# ScrapDrop: production-planning positioning research

Research date: 26 October 2026 (Malaysia). This is a review of public vendor pages, not a hands-on benchmark. Vendor capability statements are attributed claims; absence from a reviewed page does not prove a feature is missing. Suggested openings are product hypotheses to validate with users.

## Recommended focus

Position ScrapDrop as a small-batch production decision tool for packaging, paper-goods and event-material studios. Start with one repeatable production method and a participating material shop. Avoid claiming general manufacturing guidance or better industrial nesting until machine/material constraints are validated.

A proposed user question: “Can I fulfill 80 packaging units by Friday from what I have, at an acceptable margin—and what is the smallest useful purchase?”

The defensible test is a connected decision: editable finished size → material yield → labor/cash comparison → exact stock-backed purchase → measured outcome. AI can interpret briefs and explain verified tradeoffs; geometry, price, inventory and payments must remain deterministic.

## Next product hypotheses to discuss, not implemented in this update

1. **Total job economics:** separate immediate cash outlay from owned material consumption, labor, overhead, preparation and collection costs. Existing owned stock is not economically free. Compare contribution/profit estimates against a user-entered selling price, with clear assumptions.
2. **User-approved flexibility:** ask what may change (finished size, palette, finish, quantity, deadline), then show alternatives only within those limits. The current fixed 15% smaller option is a prototype, not a full search.
3. **Shared cutting across jobs:** find opportunities to place parts from several orders on the same material sheet, subject to material properties and deadlines. Add measured offcut rectangles and identification before claiming an offcut inventory loop.
4. **Production feedback:** record actual material consumption, rejected pieces and elapsed hands-on time. Compare forecasts with outcomes instead of displaying unsupported productivity or savings percentages.
5. **Commerce that closes the shortage:** keep transparent stock-backed top-ups and optional preparation central to PayPal. Multi-shop fulfillment, supplier comparison, split payments and marketplace payouts require additional implementation; the current application has one merchant.

Validate with a small studio: measure time to prepare a quote and cutting plan, new-material spend for the same accepted specification, and completion/rework against the plan. Charge hypotheses—paid preparation, recurring shop planning, or transaction fees—need willingness-to-pay interviews and unit economics; they are not proven revenue forecasts.
